## Why

Today every service visit is generated by a contract line's fixed recurrence (`start_date` + `interval_days`); there is no way to book an extra, one-off visit at a specific time a customer wants. The business wants customers to eventually pick their own open slot for such a visit self-service; for now, staff will pick it on the customer's behalf from the Customer Portal (the surface this will live on once customer authentication exists), so the workflow and its UI location don't need to change later.

## What Changes

- A new "free slots" query, scoped to a contract line, returns candidate (employee, date, start/end time) slots over the next 14 days where an employee has the contract line's required skills, is scoped to its region, has a resolved working-hours window, and has no conflicting existing visit — the same hard constraints the optimizer already respects, applied here to find open time rather than to jointly place many visits.
- Booking one of those slots creates a new service visit for the contract line at the chosen date, immediately assigned to the chosen employee at the chosen time — using the same assignment mechanics as manually assigning any other visit, so the resulting visit is indistinguishable from one created any other way except for how its date and employee were chosen.
- The Customer Portal's Contracts view gains a "Book ad-hoc visit" action per contract line that shows the available free slots and lets a user pick one. This is the one deliberate exception to the Customer Portal's existing read-only rule, and is not gated behind the customer switcher — like the rest of the Contracts view, it isn't scoped by the switcher's selection.
- **Explicitly out of scope for now** (per user direction, to keep this minimal): customer authentication/identity (the action is available to anyone who can reach the Customer Portal, same as everything else in it today), travel-time-aware slot filtering (a returned slot is time-window-feasible but not checked against driving time to/from the employee's neighboring visits), and "offers" (a separate, later concept that will also involve picking a slot but is not part of this change).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `ad-hoc-visits`: finding free slots for a contract line and booking one into an immediately assigned service visit.

### Modified Capabilities
- `customer-portal`: "Customer Portal is fully read-only" gains its one exception (booking an ad-hoc visit); a new requirement adds the "Book ad-hoc visit" action to the Contracts view.

## Impact

- `backend/app/`: new module (e.g. `ad_hoc_visits.py`) for free-slot search, reusing `resolve_employee_schedule` (`employee_schedule.py`) and the same skill/region matching the solver payload builder already does; two new endpoints (free-slot search, booking) under `contract-lines`.
- `frontend/src/customer-portal/ContractsView.tsx`, `frontend/src/api.ts`: new "Book ad-hoc visit" action, slot list, and confirmation.
- No changes to `solver/` — booked ad-hoc visits become ordinary assigned service visits and are only revisited by a later optimization run if their assignment is later unpinned.
